# Crunchy Cauliflower and Lentil Oven Bake

Serves 2 to 3

## Overview

Goal:
A hands-off, flavorful one-tray bake with crispy edges and tender centers.

Effort:
Low.

Equipment:
Baking tray, mixing bowl, knife.

Constraints:
Uses pantry staples. Minimal chopping. Forgiving timing.

Discovery:
Small-cut cauliflower cooks surprisingly well in a tomato-based bake. It softens while retaining a slight bite.

Result:
A satisfying, textured dish with minimal active work. The cauliflower stays slightly crisp while the lentils turn creamy.

## Ingredients

- 1 small cauliflower (about 500-600 g)
- 1 cup (200 g) pre-cooked green or brown lentils, drained well
- 1 jar (300-400 g) tomatoes in oil (use all the oil)
- 1-2 tbsp extra olive oil (if needed)
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- Salt and black pepper
- Optional: pinch of chili flakes, 1-2 cloves garlic (minced)

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan).

2. Cut the cauliflower into small, confetti-like pieces. A 5-second cutter works well, but a knife and patience do too.

3. In a mixing bowl, combine the cauliflower and lentils with the tomato oil, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper. Add extra olive oil if the mixture looks dry.

4. Spread everything on a baking tray in a single layer.

5. Roast for 45 minutes, stirring once or twice during cooking.

6. The dish is done when the cauliflower is tender but still retains a slight bite.

## Notes

The tomato oil provides both fat and flavor, so no additional sauce is needed.

For extra crunch, sprinkle a handful of breadcrumbs over the tray for the last 10 minutes of baking.

This dish is forgiving. If it looks dry, add a splash of water. If it needs more color, broil for 2-3 minutes at the end.

Serve straight from the tray, perhaps with a squeeze of lemon or fresh herbs.
