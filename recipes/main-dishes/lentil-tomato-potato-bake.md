# Lentil, Tomato, and Potato Bake

Serves 2 to 3

## Overview

Goal:
A beginner-proof, one-dish meal that requires minimal attention and no precise measuring.

Effort:
Low.

Equipment:
Baking dish, knife, mixing spoon.

Constraints:
Uses canned lentils.
No soaking or pre-cooking required. Works with whatever vegetables need using up.

Discovery:
Canned lentils absorb flavour surprisingly well during baking, while the potatoes provide most of the texture and substance.

Result:
A hearty, comforting bake with tender potatoes, soft lentils, and a rich tomato base. The texture varies from creamy to slightly crisp depending on the vegetables used.

## Ingredients

- 3 medium potatoes (about 500-600 g)
- 1 cup dried red lentils
- 1 jar (300-400 g) tomatoes in oil, or 1 can (400 g) chopped tomatoes plus 2 tbsp oil
- 1 small onion (optional)
- 1-2 cloves garlic (optional)
- 1-2 cups (250-500 ml) water
- Salt and black pepper
- 1 tsp smoked paprika (optional but recommended)
- Optional: 1-2 handfuls stale bread cubes
- Optional vegetables: ½ small cauliflower, kale leaves, 1 carrot, or any wilting greens

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (170°C fan).

2. Slice the potatoes thinly. They do not need to be perfect.

3. If using cauliflower, cut it into small pieces. Small pieces cook faster.

4. Rinse the lentils in a sieve.

5. In a baking dish, combine the potatoes, lentils, tomatoes with all their oil, onion, garlic (if using), and spices.

6. Add 1-2 cups of water. The mixture should look moist but not soupy. Lentils need liquid to cook properly.

7. Stir everything once to distribute the ingredients.

8. Cover the dish with a lid. If you do not have a lid, use a baking tray placed upside down on top, or reuse a piece of foil.

9. Bake for 40-50 minutes.

10. Check if the potatoes are tender. The lentils only need to be heated through.

11. Optional crisp layer: Toss kale leaves in a little oil and salt, then place on top for the last 10 minutes. Watch closely, as kale can burn quickly.

## Notes

Primary version:
Canned lentils.

Variation:
Dried lentils may be possible with adjustments.

This dish is forgiving. If it is too thick, add water. If it is too thin, bake uncovered for 5-10 minutes to reduce the liquid.

The tomatoes in oil already contain fat, which improves the mouthfeel without needing a separate sauce.

If using dried lentils instead of canned lentils, red lentils cook the fastest. Green or brown lentils may require additional liquid and a longer baking time to soften fully.

Small-cut vegetables reduce the cognitive load later. The smaller the pieces, the less you need to think about whether they are cooked through.
