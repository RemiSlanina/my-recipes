# Lemon Basil Pasta

## Overview

Goal:
A fresh pasta dish with bright flavours and minimal ingredients.

Effort:
Low.

Equipment:
Large pot, frying pan.

Constraints:
Works with pantry ingredients and a single fresh herb.

Discovery:
A small amount of pasta water improves the texture of the sauce significantly.

Result:
Creamy lemon pasta with basil and a surprisingly rich flavour despite its simplicity.

## Ingredients

- 400 g pasta (such as penne)
- 1 onion
- 1 organic lemon
- 250 ml vegan cream (cuisine)
- 2 handfuls fresh basil
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt
- Black pepper

## Method

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.

2. Cook the pasta until al dente.

3. Meanwhile, finely dice the onion.

4. Grate the zest from the lemon and juice half of it.

5. Heat olive oil in a frying pan.

6. Cook the onion until soft.

7. Add the lemon zest and lemon juice.

8. Stir briefly.

9. Add the vegan cream and chopped basil.

10. Simmer gently for a few minutes.

11. Reserve a small cup of pasta water.

12. Drain the pasta and add it to the sauce.

13. Add a splash of pasta water and mix thoroughly.

14. Season with salt and pepper.

15. Serve immediately.

## Notes

The lemon should be noticeable but not overwhelming.

Additional pasta water can be added to loosen the sauce if needed.

Fresh basil works significantly better than dried basil in this recipe.

This recipe sits somewhere between comfort food and summer food.
