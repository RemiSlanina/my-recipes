# Cold Kidney Beans

Serves 1

## Overview

Goal:
A quick pantry meal that turns plain kidney beans into a flavorful Mediterranean-style dish with almost no preparation.

Effort:
Very low.

Equipment:
Bowl and spoon.

Constraints:
No cooking required. Uses shelf-stable ingredients.

Discovery:
Basil and roasted onions complement kidney beans surprisingly well. Olive oil rounds out the earthy flavor, while [balsamic vinegar](https://m.dict.cc/englisch-deutsch/balsamic+vinegar.html) adds brightness.

Result:
A simple, savory bean salad that works as a light meal or side dish.

## Ingredients

- 1 can kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 to 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 to 2 tsp [balsamic vinegar](https://m.dict.cc/englisch-deutsch/balsamic+vinegar.html) (to taste)
- 1 tsp fresh basil
- 1 to 2 tbsp roasted onions
- Optional: ½ tsp dried oregano, or adjust the herb blend to taste
- Salt and pepper, if needed

## Method

1. Drain and rinse the kidney beans.
2. Combine all ingredients in a bowl.
3. Mix well and let stand for 5 to 10 minutes, if time allows, so the basil softens and the flavors blend.

## Notes

The olive oil softens the beans' earthy character, while the vinegar provides contrast. Roasted onions add sweetness and texture.

For a more substantial meal, serve with bread, rice, pasta, or another grain.

Kidney beans are relatively low in methionine. Pairing them with grains provides a more complementary amino acid profile.
