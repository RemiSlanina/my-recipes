# Patate e Porri al Forno

## Overview

Goal:
Comfort food with minimal active work.

Effort:
Low to Medium.

Equipment:
Knife, baking dish, oven.

Constraints:
Can be assembled in advance.
Works with inexpensive ingredients.
Flexible vegetable choices.

Discovery:
Thinly sliced potatoes cook more reliably than thick slices and require no pre-cooking.

Result:
A smoky potato and leek bake with vegan béchamel.

## Ingredients

### Base

- 500 g potatoes
- ½ leek
- 2-3 carrots, grated
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- Salt to taste

### Vegan Béchamel

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp flour
- 500 ml oat milk (or water)
- Salt
- Black pepper
- Nutmeg
- 1 bay leaf (optional)

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.

2. Slice the potatoes as thinly as practical.

3. Wash and slice the leek.

4. Place potatoes, leek, and carrots in a bowl.

5. Add soy sauce, olive oil, smoked paprika, and salt. Mix thoroughly.

6. Transfer to a baking dish.

### Prepare the Béchamel

1. Heat olive oil in a small saucepan.

2. Add flour and stir for 1-2 minutes.

3. Gradually add oat milk while whisking.

4. Add salt, pepper, nutmeg, and bay leaf.

5. Simmer until thickened.

6. Remove bay leaf.

### Assemble

1. Pour the béchamel over the vegetables.

2. Cover the dish with a lid or foil.

3. Bake for approximately 45 minutes.

4. Remove the cover for the final 10 minutes if a lightly coloured top is desired.

## Notes

This recipe tolerates substitutions well.

Additional vegetables such as zucchini, spinach, or cabbage can be added.

A green salad works particularly well alongside the richness of the béchamel.

The combination of smoked paprika and soy sauce contributes much of the savoury flavour.
