# Spiced Eggplant and Bean Moussaka

Serves 2 to 4

## Overview

Goal:
Low-effort vegan moussaka.

Effort:
Medium.

Equipment:
Microwave, frying pan, oven dish.

Constraints:
Limited energy.
Minimal chopping.
No potato preparation.

Discovery:
Microwaving salted eggplant produces acceptable texture.

Result:
Spiced Eggplant and Bean Moussaka.

## Ingredients

2 medium eggplants (melanzani)
1 large zucchini
1 can kidney beans, drained
1 jar or 400-500 g spicy tomato sauce (such as arrabbiata)
1 onion, diced
2 to 4 tablespoons olive oil
1 teaspoon harissa, or to taste
A generous pinch of nutmeg
Salt and black pepper

For the topping:

150-200 ml almond cream
A little extra nutmeg
Salt and pepper

To serve:

Fresh salad
Good bread

## Method

Place sliced eggplant in a microwave-safe bowl with a splash of water and a pinch of salt. Cover loosely and microwave until softened, about 4 to 8 minutes depending on quantity and microwave power.

Meanwhile, gently fry the onion in olive oil until soft.

Add the zucchini and cook for a few minutes.

Add the tomato sauce, kidney beans, harissa, nutmeg, salt, and pepper. Simmer briefly until everything comes together.

Fold in the softened eggplant.

Transfer to an oven dish if needed.

Season the almond cream with salt, pepper, and a little nutmeg. Spread over the top.

Bake at 180°C until bubbling and lightly coloured, about 20 to 30 minutes.

Allow to rest for 5 to 10 minutes before serving.

Serve with a fresh salad and bread.

## Notes

The eggplant microwave step replaces the traditional roasting stage and dramatically reduces effort while still producing a soft, silky texture.

Bread is recommended instead of potatoes. Potatoes are traditional in some moussakas but significantly increase preparation work while contributing relatively little to the overall character of the dish.

The flavour balance should be warm, earthy, slightly spicy, and comforting. The nutmeg should be noticeable but not dominant.

If the mixture seems too wet, simmer the tomato sauce a little longer before assembling.
