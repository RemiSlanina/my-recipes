# Celery Salad with Lemon

Serves 4

## Overview

Goal:
A crisp, refreshing salad with minimal ingredients and effort.

Effort:
Low.

Equipment:
Salad bowl, grater.

Constraints:
Uses pantry staples plus one fresh vegetable. Can be made ahead.

Discovery:
The lemon juice prevents the celeriac from browning and adds a bright contrast to the earthy flavor.

Result:
A simple, crunchy salad that pairs well with rich or heavy dishes.

## Ingredients

- 1 celeriac (celery root), about 500-600 g
- 1 organic lemon
- Salt
- Black pepper or lemon pepper

## Method

1. Wash, peel, and grate the celeriac.

2. Juice the lemon.

3. Combine the grated celeriac and lemon juice in a salad bowl.

4. Season with salt and pepper to taste.

## Notes

Use an organic lemon if you want to include some zest for extra flavor.

This salad keeps well for a day if stored with the lemon juice, though the texture softens slightly over time.

For variation, add a drizzle of olive oil or a pinch of fresh herbs like parsley or dill.
