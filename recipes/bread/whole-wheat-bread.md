# Whole Wheat Bread

## Overview

Goal:
Create a simple homemade bread with a softer texture than typical dense whole wheat loaves.

Effort:
Medium.

Equipment:
Mixing bowl, oven or bread machine.

Constraints:
Limited access to specialty flours.
Attempting to recreate the texture of Croatian and Italian breads using commonly available healthy ingredients.

Discovery:
A mixture of white flour and whole wheat flour produces a lighter texture than whole wheat flour alone.

Result:
Versatile everyday bread that can be baked in an oven or bread machine.

## Ingredients

- 280 ml lukewarm water
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 200 g white wheat flour
- 300 g whole wheat flour
- ½ tsp bread spice (optional)
- Seeds to taste (sunflower, flaxseed, pumpkin seeds, etc.)
- 7 g dry yeast (1 packet)

OR

- ½ cube fresh yeast (approximately 20 g)

## Method (Oven)

1. Combine the flours, bread spice, and salt in a large bowl.

2. Add the yeast.

3. Pour in the lukewarm water and olive oil.

4. Knead for 5-10 minutes until a smooth dough forms.

5. Cover with a clean kitchen towel or lid and leave to rise in a warm place for about 1 hour.

6. Knead briefly again and shape into a loaf.

7. Place on a baking tray lined with baking paper or transfer to a loaf tin.

8. Cover and allow to rise for another 30-60 minutes.

9. Preheat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan).

10. Score the top of the loaf with a sharp knife.

11. Place a heatproof dish filled with water on the lower oven rack to create steam.

12. Bake for 45-60 minutes until the loaf is browned and sounds hollow when tapped on the bottom.

13. Allow to cool before slicing.

## Method (Bread Machine)

Follow the manufacturer's instructions.

A common order is:

1. Water
2. Olive oil
3. Flour
4. Seeds
5. Salt and yeast added separately

For dry yeast, dissolve the salt in the water and sprinkle the yeast on top of the flour.

For fresh yeast, dissolve the yeast in the lukewarm water and add the salt separately.

## Notes

The recipe also works with other flour combinations, including spelt flour and whole wheat spelt flour.

Rye flour generally produces a heavier loaf and often benefits from the addition of sourdough.

The exact amount of water may vary depending on the flour used. Adjust as needed until the dough is soft but manageable.
