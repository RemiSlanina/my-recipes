# Flatbread (Pan-Fried)

## Overview

Goal:
Breakfast. No bread at home.

Effort:
Low.

Equipment:
Mixing bowl, frying pan.

Constraints:
No yeast.
Few ingredients.

Discovery:
Resting the dough briefly improves texture.

Result:
Quick pan-fried flatbread.

## Ingredients

- 400 g wheat flour
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 220-250 ml warm water
- 1 pinch salt (optional)

## Instructions

1. Place the flour, olive oil, and salt (if using) in a mixing bowl.

2. Gradually add the warm water while mixing until a soft dough forms.

3. Knead the dough for several minutes until smooth.

4. Let the dough rest for 5-10 minutes.

5. Knead briefly once more, then divide and shape into flat rounds.

6. Heat a non-stick frying pan over medium heat.

7. Cook the flatbreads without additional oil until golden brown on both sides and cooked through.

8. Serve warm.

## Notes

The exact amount of water depends on the flour used. Add a little more if the dough feels dry, or a little flour if it becomes sticky.

This bread pairs well with dips, soups, salads, and vegetable dishes.
