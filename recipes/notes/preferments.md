# Preferments

## Why I Care

During the bread hunt I repeatedly encountered recipes using
poolish or biga.

Both are preferments: part of the dough is prepared
in advance and allowed to ferment before the final dough is mixed.

## Poolish

100 g flour
100 g water
tiny amount of yeast

Hydration:
100%

Texture:
Liquid, batter-like.

Expected Results:

- lighter crumb
- larger holes
- milder flavour

## Biga

100 g flour
50-60 g water
tiny amount of yeast

Hydration:
50-60%

Texture:
Firm dough.

Expected Results:

- chewier crumb
- stronger flavour
- common in Italian baking
